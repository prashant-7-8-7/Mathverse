Our Legacy & Vision

The Story of Our Mathematics Club

From a whiteboard circle in Science Hall to a real-time living universe of competitive mathematics, peer research, and intellectual camaraderie.

Mathematics as a Living, Expanding Cosmos.

Founded in 2018 by seven undergraduate mathematicians and physicists in a chalkboard-walled corner of Room 402, our Mathematics Club began with informal puzzle hours and Putnam problem circles. Over eight years, it has transformed into a 500+ member student powerhouse.

We believe mathematical intuition isn't a solitary talent—it thrives in community. Whether breaking down differential geometry, analyzing stochastic quantum models, or locking horns in a buzzer-lightning integration sprint, our members push each other to think bolder and deeper.
