How Mathematics Club Works

From reserving a seat at an Integration Bee to holding a verified championship credential.

STEP 01 🎫

Register

Browse upcoming club symposiums, workshops, and Integration Bees. Claim your seat and instantly receive a cryptographic pass with QR code.

STEP 02 📷

Check In

Arrive at the event venue. Admins authenticate your pass via device camera scan or instant code lookup at the door with zero queues.

STEP 03 ⚡

Play in the Arena

Join the live multiplayer speed tournament from your phone or laptop. Solve KaTeX-rendered mathematical problems under real-time pressure.

STEP 04 🏆

Climb the Leaderboard

Earn speed multipliers and precision points. Ascend the semester season leaderboard and unlock custom achievement badges on your profile.

STEP 05 📜

Earn Your Certificate

Receive cryptographically verifiable, high-resolution PDF certificates with QR verification links to prove your mathematics distinction.
